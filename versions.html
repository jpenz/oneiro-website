First attempt at showing the whole band. Pure text-to-video, so the model invented its own musicians. Camera reveals bouzouki → keys → guitar → drums in sequence. HDR look is great, but the faces aren't recognizable as the real band.

Face continuity + the new energy

Upgrade to full Veo 3 (10× the credits) for higher quality. Seeded on the real Oneiro photo, with a richer prompt: champagne glasses raised across the dance floor, gold confetti, rose petals, pyrotechnics. Same bouzouki player's face preserved.

"Passion of girls kissing" — first attempt

Veo 3 full · image-to-video

Prompted explicitly for two women kissing center-frame, but Veo defaulted to a man-and-woman couple even with "two women" in the prompt. Otherwise on-brand: confetti, rose petals, pyrotechnics, bouzouki in shot.

V5 · LIVE

Two women, sustained kiss — the current hero

Veo 3 full · image-to-video

More explicit prompt ("both feminine, both in dresses, female-female couple, lesbian kiss") finally landed. Two women in red and champagne-gold dresses kissing center-frame for several seconds. Bouzouki player visible behind them. A tiny rainbow bracelet appears on one of the raised hands.

V6 · ALL 4 FACES

All four members with their actual faces

Nano Banana Edit (composite) → Veo 3 full (animation)

Two-step pipeline. Step 1: Google Nano Banana Edit composited the four real band portraits — Andreas, Yianni, Dimitris, Sotirios — into a single club-stage scene preserving each man's face. Step 2: the composite seeded Veo 3 image-to-video to bring it to life — guitar strumming, keys playing, drums hitting, bouzouki picking — with red & blue stage lights, confetti, a smoke burst, and the dance floor of women in front raising champagne flutes.
